# Nordic Portfolio Atlas — SPN & SFT

Ultra-clean minimal interactive WebGL vector building & industrial footprint atlas for **Folketrygdfondet's** two Nordic equity portfolios:
- **SPN (Statens pensjonsfond Norge)**: All **151 equity holdings** (~258.5B NOK)
- **SFT (Statens fond i Tromsø)**: All **204 equity holdings** (~14.6B NOK)
- **355 Total Equity Positions** across **334 unique Nordic companies** (`SPN`, `SFT`, and `SPN+SFT` dual-held companies)
- **410 Operational Facilities**:
  - **Offshore Oil & Gas Drilling Platforms, FPSOs & Refineries**: Johan Sverdrup, Troll A, Gullfaks C, Oseberg, Snorre A, Åsgard, Johan Castberg FPSO, Melkøya LNG, Mongstad, Kårstø, Kollsnes, Valhall, Alvheim, Ivar Aasen, Skarv, Edvard Grieg, Goliat, Balder/Jotun, Draugen, Brage, Tyra II, Deepsea Atlantic, Deepsea Nordkapp, Aker Solutions Stord/Verdal/Egersund, Subsea 7 Vigra/Dusavik, Neste Porvoo
  - **Marine Fish Farms & Salmon Mega-Plants**: Mowi (Hitra Ulvan, Herøy, Hjelmeland, Valsneset, Rosyth), SalMar (InnovaMar, InnovaNor, Ocean Farm 1), Bakkafrost (Glyvrar, Havsbrún, Strond), Austevoll / Lerøy Seafood Group, Grieg Seafood, Salmon Evolution, BioMar, Brim, Síldarvinnslan
  - **Mines, Quarries, Aluminum/Copper/Zinc Smelters & Pulp Mills**: Norsk Hydro (Sunndal, Årdal, Høyanger, Karmøy, Husnes), Boliden (Aitik, Garpenberg, Rönnskär, Harjavalta, Kokkola, Odda), Elkem, Yara (Herøya, Glomfjord, Siilinjärvi phosphate mine, Uusikaupunki), Borregaard, UPM, SCA, Stora Enso, Viscaria, Sotkamo Silver, Endomines
  - **Data Centers, Teleports & Cloud**: Tietoevry, Atea, Equinor, Síminn, Bredband2, NNIT, freenet / Media Broadcast Usingen
  - **Defense, Aerospace, Wind & Industrial Factories**: Kongsberg Gruppen, Saab (Linköping, Karlskoga, Karlskrona), Volvo Torslanda, Vestas (Lindø, Nakskov, Ringkøbing), NKT Karlskrona, AutoStore, Epiroc, Atlas Copco, Alfa Laval, Wärtsilä, KONE, Konecranes, Metso
  - **Central Distribution Mega-Warehouses & Regional Offices**: DSV (Hedehusene, Landskrona, Vestby), Orkla, Kesko Hakkila, Tokmanni Mäntsälä, Europris Moss, Kid Lier, Carlsberg Fredericia, DNB, Equinor, SpareBank 1, Norconsult, Veidekke
- **Company Logos**: High-resolution company logos in the sidebar, inspector sheet, and interactive floating map markers (`Logos` toggle).
- **Zero-API-Key Vector Building Engine**: Powered by MapLibre GL JS + OpenFreeMap (`tiles.openfreemap.org`) with exact vector building footprint matching and 3D roof extrusions.
